savoury and rich flavour of ramen

Ramen is a popular Japanese noodle soup featuring alkaline wheat noodles in a flavorful broth (often soy sauce, miso, salt, or pork bone-based), topped with ingredients like sliced pork (chashu), seaweed (nori), bamboo shoots (menma), and green onions, creating a deeply savory and customizable comfort food with endless regional variations.
